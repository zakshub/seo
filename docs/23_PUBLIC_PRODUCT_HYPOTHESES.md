# Public Product Hypotheses

These are hypothesis families for comparison, not preselected winners and not claims of validated demand.

## Candidate families

1. **Beginner knowledge or glossary property.** Potentially broad long-tail content and internal-link structure; risks include intense competition, low differentiation, and high editorial maintenance.
2. **Focused technical SEO utilities.** Potential repeat use and link earning; risks include data/API dependence, accuracy obligations, crowded free-tool SERPs, and maintenance cost.
3. **Troubleshooting evidence library.** Converts recurring indexing, canonical, crawling, and visibility problems into diagnostic paths; risks include fragmented intent and overlap with official documentation/forums.
4. **Interactive SEO learning or lab.** Could combine education with practical tasks and progress; risks include high curriculum/product cost and unproven search acquisition.
5. **SEO experiment/research publication.** Could create original data, citations, backlinks, and defensibility; risks include slow evidence production and rigorous methodology requirements.
6. **Regional or Urdu SEO education/tooling.** May address underserved language needs; demand, commercial value, query language, and market size are UNKNOWN and must not be assumed.

For every actual candidate the system must replace generic assumptions with: audience, problem, representative queries, search surfaces, competitor observations, product gap, content/tool/research potential, traffic pattern, build and maintenance complexity, possible monetization, defensibility, risks, unknowns, and evidence-linked recommendation.

Current public Q&A evidence can generate candidate problem areas but cannot declare any family the winner. Phase 2 begins only after the criteria in `25_PHASE_2_ENTRY_CRITERIA.md` are met and the owner explicitly approves one direction.

# Vision and Product Constitution

The product operates a portfolio of web ventures from opportunity discovery through learning. It serves a non-SEO-expert owner, so every technical term in the Control Center needs a short plain-English explanation.

Truthfulness is constitutional: status derives from persisted workflow/events; measurements retain provenance; confidence and unknowns are visible; and a completed label means the underlying action completed. Chief Intelligence coordinates but cannot override policy. Human control can start, pause, resume, stop, inspect, approve, reject, request changes, alter operating mode, set limits, disable agents, and pause projects.

The system may recommend content sites, directories, calculators, comparison products, databases, discovery products, marketplaces, utilities, or micro SaaS, but does not presuppose a product form. SEO claims must distinguish official guidance, established principles, observed patterns, hypotheses, and validated learning.

# End-to-End Workflow

Opportunity discovery creates evidence records, then research enriches and scores it. Product strategy produces a transparent scorecard. Chief Intelligence selects candidates for review. Owner approval creates a Project and queues design preparation; it never creates a Figma artifact implicitly. Later stages are design, review, approval, build, QA, deployment approval, deployment, measurement, growth, finance, experiments, and learning.

Every transition is a command evaluated against workflow state, policy, permission, budget, evidence completeness, and any required approval. Pause prevents new task dispatch; stop marks cancellable work and records outcomes without deleting history. Failures are terminal only when retry policy is exhausted or an operator rejects continuation.

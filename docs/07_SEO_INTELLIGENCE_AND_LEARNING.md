# SEO Intelligence and Learning

Research evaluates search demand signals, intent, SERP composition, competitors, gaps, commercial fit, feasibility, and confidence. A lack of an authorized source is recorded as unknown, not inferred as favorable. Public-source access is rate-limited, policy-controlled, and provenance-rich.

Phase 1.5 formalizes provider-independent capabilities and the modern opportunity equation: demand + intent + SERP reality + competitor quality + content/product gap + topic traffic potential + click potential + solution fit + authority requirement + build feasibility + strategic value. Stack Exchange engagement and Wikimedia article pageviews are directional observations, never search volume. Google Trends, live SERPs and Search Console remain unavailable until verified access exists. See `26_PHASE_1_5_MARKET_EVIDENCE.md` and ADR 0007.

Volume, KD, DA, DR, backlinks, rankings and traffic must come from a named lawful source. Historical numeric thresholds are HEURISTICS only and are never promoted to laws or imputed from missing data.

Knowledge records are official source, established principle, observed pattern, project-specific finding, hypothesis, experiment, validated learning, or deprecated learning. Each has provenance, confidence, scope, review date, and evidence links. Experiments require a hypothesis, success measure, guardrail, decision rule, and final outcome before becoming validated learning.

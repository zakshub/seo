# SEO Intelligence and Learning

Research evaluates search demand signals, intent, SERP composition, competitors, gaps, commercial fit, feasibility, and confidence. A lack of an authorized source is recorded as unknown, not inferred as favorable. Public-source access is rate-limited, policy-controlled, and provenance-rich.

Knowledge records are official source, established principle, observed pattern, project-specific finding, hypothesis, experiment, validated learning, or deprecated learning. Each has provenance, confidence, scope, review date, and evidence links. Experiments require a hypothesis, success measure, guardrail, decision rule, and final outcome before becoming validated learning.

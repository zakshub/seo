# Automation and Approval Modes

Manual requires approval for consequential transitions. Supervised lets allowed tasks continue but stops at strategic, financial, external, or irreversible decisions. Auto Pilot continues only within explicit grants, budgets, and risk limits.

All modes hard-gate production-destructive actions, DNS, domain purchases, paid services, unexpected spend, secret access, major infrastructure changes, and high-risk business categories. Approval requests snapshot the proposed command, evidence, policy decision, cost estimate, and expiry. Approval is single-use, scoped, auditable, and invalidated if material inputs change.
